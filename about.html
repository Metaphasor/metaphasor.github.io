About Metaphasor

Metaphasor is an independent artificial intelligence company founded by Chris Speciale.

The founder

Chris has spent his career building the tools other developers build on. He helps maintain OpenFL and Lime, open-source frameworks used to create games and applications across many platforms, and through Dimensionscape he works on compilers, runtimes and developer tooling across the Haxe ecosystem.

That work has given him a close, first-hand view of how software actually gets made: by many hands, across many projects, and increasingly with AI doing part of the work. It has also shown him where today's ways of working start to break down.

Why Metaphasor

Metaphasor grew out of that experience. We believe the way software is built is about to change more in the next few years than it has in decades, and we intend to help lead that change.

How we work

We are research-led. Every idea starts as careful design and written research, and is tested against real-world work before it ships. We are independent, we take the long view, and we build quietly until there is something worth showing.
